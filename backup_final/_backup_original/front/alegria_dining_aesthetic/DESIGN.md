## Brand & Style

The brand personality embodies refined warmth, timeless hospitality, and artisanal culinary prestige. Tailored for discerning diners, epicureans, and guests seeking an intimate, elevated gastronomic escape, the interface must project quiet confidence and sensorial indulgence.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with **Tactile/Warm Editorial Luxury**:
- Generous, expansive negative space reminiscent of high-end editorial menus.
- Rich ivory and warm cream foundations avoiding cold clinical whites.
- Subtle golden-amber metallic glows, fine hairlines, and sun-warmed lighting cues.
- High-contrast espresso typography paired with graceful classical serifs to create an atmosphere of deliberate, unhurried fine dining.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from roasted espresso beans, clarified butter, aged parchment, and polished brass tableware.

- **Primary (`#C59B27`)**: Rich antique amber gold. Reserved for key calls to action, badges of culinary distinction, subtle linear accents, and active selection states.
- **Secondary (`#7B3F00`)**: Deep burnt sienna and roasted cacao. Lends rich warmth to secondary interactive states, category badges, and hover depth.
- **Tertiary (`#8C6D53`)**: Muted warm taupe and hazelnut. Used for metadata, borders, ornamental rules, and iconography.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FDFBF7`)**: Warm ivory foundation. Complemented by layered tones:
  - Surface base: `#FDFBF7` (Ivory canvas)
  - Surface container low: `#F7F3EB` (Warm cream card fill)
  - Surface container high: `#EFE9DC` (Warm sandstone for hovering elements and menu dividers)
  - Text & Headers: `#1F1A17` (Deep espresso charcoal, avoiding stark pitch black for a softer, organic presence)
  - Text secondary: `#5C5248` (Smoked umber for ingredient lists and descriptions)

## Typography

The typography pairing contrasts the artisanal heritage of transitional serifs with modern, human-centric sans-serif readability.

- **Playfair Display** commands headlines, section titles, and course names. It evokes printed carte du jour tradition, fine dining menus, and European culinary publications.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides clarity and warmth across body text, reservation parameters, ingredient tasting notes, and interactive actions.
- Uppercase tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.08em - 0.1em`) applies exclusively to badges, category tags, tasting flight steps, and micro-labels to convey curated elegance.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive grid ensures generous framing for culinary photography and curated menus.

- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (up to 767px, 4 columns), Tablet (768px - 1023px, 8 columns), Desktop (1024px and above, 12 columns).
- **Rhythm**: Editorial density demands expansive margins and generous vertical air. Section dividers use `space-xl` combined with delicate warm-gold separator lines.
- **Alignment**: Food showcases and signature pairings feature asymmetric layouts, alternating full-bleed imagery with offset typography cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through soft, amber-tinted ambient shadows and tonal ivory-to-cream layering, strictly avoiding clinical grey drop shadows.

- **Resting Cards**: Supported by a delicate 1px border (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.15)`) over `rgba(247, 243, 235, 0.8)` with shadow `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(92, 82, 72, 0.06)`.
- **Elevated / Interactive (Menu Highlights, Reservation Drawers)**: Shadow shifts to an illuminated glow: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(123, 63, 0, 0.08), 0 0 16px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.1)`.
- **Glassmorphism Overlay**: Navigation bars and modal overlays use `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over a warm tint (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85)`), allowing background dish photography to glow subtly through.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, architectural shape language (Level 1). 

- Standard inputs, buttons, and food cards feature refined 4px (`0.25rem`) rounded corners, projecting structured elegance rather than playful informality.
- Featured hero imagery, tasting highlight frames, and dietary badges may scale to 8px (`rounded-lg`) for soft framing without breaking architectural precision.
- Decorative accent elements (e.g., wine pairing tags) retain clean rectilinear balance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Table Reservation, Order Curated Box)**: Deep espresso or golden amber fill (`#C59B27`) with warm ivory typography (`#FDFBF7`). Soft gold linear sheen on hover with a 2px lift.
- **Secondary (Explore Menu, Wine Cellar)**: Transparent background, 1px border in amber bronze (`#C59B27`), espresso text, smooth color inversion on hover.
- **Text Action**: Playfair Display italic or tracked small caps with an animated golden underline expanding from center.

### Cards (Menu Items & Seasonal Highlights)
- Flat or subtly elevated warm cream surface (`#F7F3EB`) framed by an ultra-thin hairline border (`rgba(140, 109, 83, 0.2)`).
- Food photography uses high aspect ratios (4:3 or 16:9) with slow 1.03x zoom transitions on hover.
- Price tags are set in distinct, un-bolded serif typography aligned dynamically with dish titles.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- Compact padding (`0.25rem 0.75rem`), subtle parchment background, fine gold outline.
- Labels rendered in tracked `label-sm` font for Michelin-guide style notes (e.g., "CHEF'S SELECTION", "LOCALLY SOURCED", "PAIRING AVAILABLE").

### Input Fields & Reservation Pickers
- Flush cream containers with bottom-line accent or full subtle borders (`rgba(92, 82, 72, 0.2)`).
- On focus, border transitions smoothly to luminous golden amber (`#C59B27`) with an ambient warm glow.
- Calendar and guest selectors use warm typography with circular selection rings in gold.

### Lists & Menu Ledgers
- Editorial split layout: dish title and ingredients on the left, dotted brass leader line, price anchored to the right.
- Allergens and tasting notes nested beneath in muted hazelnut (`#8C6D53`).

### Additional Hospitality Components
- **Wine Pairing Drawer**: Collapsible card with sommelier notes, terroir highlights, and temperature suggestions.
- **Chef's Tasting Timeline**: Vertical step indicator traced in brass leaf accents detailing multi-course dining progress.
